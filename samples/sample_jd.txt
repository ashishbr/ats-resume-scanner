Software Engineer, Payments Platform

About the role
We are a fintech company building real-time payment processing systems that move billions of dollars a year. You will design and build backend services for our payments platform, work with product and risk teams, and own features from design to production.

What you'll do
- Design, build and operate scalable microservices in Python and Java
- Build REST APIs and event-driven pipelines with Kafka
- Deploy services on AWS using Docker and Kubernetes
- Improve observability, reliability and latency of payment flows
- Partner with fraud and risk teams on fraud detection signals

Requirements
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or a related field
- 2+ years of experience in backend software development
- Strong proficiency in Python or Java
- Experience with SQL and PostgreSQL
- Experience with REST APIs and microservices
- Hands-on experience with AWS, Docker and CI/CD
- Solid understanding of data structures, algorithms and distributed systems
- Strong communication and collaboration skills

Preferred qualifications
- Experience with Kafka or other message queues
- Experience with Kubernetes and Terraform
- Familiarity with payment processing, PCI compliance or fraud detection
- Experience with Redis caching
- Master's degree in Computer Science or Information Technology

We are an equal opportunity employer and value diversity. All qualified applicants will receive consideration without regard to race, color, religion, sex, national origin, disability or veteran status.
Salary range: $110,000 - $140,000 plus benefits.
